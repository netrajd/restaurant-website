Our Heritage & Craft

Rooted in Earth, Perfected by Flame

Founded in 2012 by Executive Chef Nicolas Vance and Master Baker Elena Rossi, Aura & Hearth was born from a singular passion: returning to honest, soulful culinary traditions.

Every morning begins at 5:00 AM with the crackle of seasoned oak in our custom stone hearth. We partner directly with 18 local heritage farms to harvest seasonal produce at peak ripeness. From our 48-hour fermented sourdough loaves to slow-braised cuts, every dish tells a story of care and patience.
